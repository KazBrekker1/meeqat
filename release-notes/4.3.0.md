- The real sky behind the Moon: the stars, constellations and planets where they really are right now from your location, with the Moon in its true place among them
- Tap the Moon to explore the whole sky: drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom, and tap a star, planet or constellation for its name in English and Arabic
- Search the sky in English or Arabic, move through the day with a time slider that stops at each prayer, and find the Qibla marked on the horizon
