### New
- Android widget redesigned to match the app: the glass moon, a clearer countdown, and layouts that fit every size without cut-off times
- Widget times follow your 12h/24h setting
- A note under "Today's prayers" when the chosen city is in a different timezone than your device
- Pick a date in the calendar to see that day's prayer times, without changing today's
- The update dialog now tells you what actually changed

### Improved
- Much lighter on battery on Android: the widget no longer runs a background service or shows a permanent notification
- The widget keeps a week of prayer times, so it stays correct for days without opening the app
- Faster start: the map loads only when you open it
- Icons are built in, so the app looks right offline
- Android TV boxes with 32-bit processors are supported

### Fixed
- Prayer reminders stopped when you opened Rooms or Privacy; they now keep running everywhere
- After choosing a new city, reminders were only scheduled for today
- Android reminders could arrive late while the phone was idle
- Browsing the calendar could replace today's times in the list, tray and widget
- Switching cities quickly could show one city's times under another's name
- The orbit kept the previous city's prayer positions
- Widget showing the wrong city when using your location, and stale dates after midnight
- No reminder for a Pray Together call finalised less than 5 minutes ahead
- Security hardening for the in-app updater and the desktop app
